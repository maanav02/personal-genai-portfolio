01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Why this project?

Generative AI is easy to access, but using it effectively requires more than typing a question. This project explores how to ask better questions, assess responses, and apply AI thoughtfully.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Using AI is not the same as understanding AI.

Students may rely on AI-generated content without understanding how the tools work, how prompts influence outputs, or how to identify inaccurate information.

This portfolio addresses that gap through guided tool exploration, structured prompt experiments, output comparison, and fact verification.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES
  • Understand AI, ML, deep learning and GenAI.
  • Explore and compare at least three AI tools.
  • Apply five prompt engineering techniques.
  • Improve prompts through iteration and context.
  • Verify outputs and practise responsible AI use.
02 / CORE KNOWLEDGE

AI fundamentals

A quick reference to the concepts behind modern AI systems. Use these explanations as a starting point and cite the sources you consult in your final report.

01
◈

Artificial Intelligence

The broad field of building systems that perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as reasoning, perception, and language understanding.

The broader field
02
⌁

Machine Learning

A subset of AI in which systems learn patterns from data to make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed for every case.

Learning from data
03
▦

Deep Learning

A type of machine learning that uses multi-layered neural networks to learn complex patterns from data such as images, audio, and text.

Neural networks
04
✳

Generative AI

AI systems that generate new content—such as text, images, audio, video, or code—based on patterns learned during training.

Creating new content
Aa

Large Language Models (LLMs)

Models trained on large amounts of text that can process and generate language. They produce responses by predicting likely next tokens based on the input and context.

▤

Tokens & context windows

Tokens are pieces of text processed by a model. A context window is the amount of input and conversation context a model can consider at one time. Tokenisation and limits vary by model.

✦

Important distinction: Generative AI is not limited to text or LLMs. It also includes models that generate images, audio, video, and other forms of content.

04 / PROMPT ENGINEERING

The prompt lab

A prompt is an instruction or input given to an AI system. Specificity, context, examples, and iteration can influence the usefulness of the response.

01ZERO-SHOT

Ask without an example

Give the model a task directly, without showing a sample answer.

TRY THISAnalyze the supplied student-portal email and decide whether it is potentially phishing or legitimate. Identify warning signs and recommend safe actions. State when evidence is insufficient.
Observe: Does the response follow all requested parts?
02ONE-SHOT

Guide with one example

Provide one example of the format or pattern you want the model to follow.

TRY THISExample: Your bank account will be blocked. Enter your password immediately using this link. Classification: Potential phishing. Reason: Urgency and a request for sensitive credentials. Now classify the supplied student-portal email and give warning signs and recommended actions.
Observe: Does the answer mirror the example?
03FEW-SHOT

Provide multiple examples

Show several input-output examples to establish a pattern before the task.

TRY THISUse three example email classifications (phishing, routine academic notice, and prize scam) to establish a pattern. Then classify the supplied student-portal email and explain which example it most closely matches.
Observe: Does it apply the pattern consistently?
04ROLE-BASED

Assign a useful role

Set a perspective, audience, and communication style for the task.

TRY THISAct as an experienced cybersecurity analyst specialising in email security. Analyse the supplied student-portal email, identify suspicious indicators and potential risks, recommend safe actions, and distinguish confirmed evidence from assumptions.
Observe: Is the depth and tone suitable for the audience?
05CONTEXTUAL + ITERATIVE

Give context, then refine

Start with a broad request, add relevant background, and ask for a targeted revision.

INITIAL PROMPTIs this email phishing? Your student portal access will be disabled today. Confirm your password using the link below to avoid suspension.
↓
FOLLOW-UP / IMPROVED PROMPTI am a first-year B.Tech CSE student learning cybersecurity and phishing detection. Improve your previous analysis. Explain warning signs, distinguish confirmed evidence from assumptions, and suggest safe verification steps. Organise the response into classification, suspicious indicators, potential risks, and recommended actions. Use beginner-friendly language and avoid unsupported claims.
Observe: What improved after context and feedback were added?
✦

Prompt improvement tip: State the task, audience, context, constraints, desired format, and quality criteria. Ask follow-up questions when the first response is incomplete.

07 / RESPONSIBLE AI

Trust, but verify.

AI-generated responses can be fluent and still be incorrect. Responsible use means checking important claims, protecting private information, and applying human judgement.

!

Hallucinations

AI may generate false facts, invented references, or confident explanations that are not supported by evidence.

◉

Bias & fairness

Outputs may reflect biases in training data or framing. Consider whose perspectives are missing.

⌑

Privacy

Do not enter passwords, personal identifiers, confidential records, or sensitive information into AI tools.

✓

Human oversight

Check important outputs against trustworthy sources and take responsibility for what you submit or publish.

VERIFICATION WORKFLOW

Five checks before trusting an output

  1. 01 Identify factual claims that need checking.
  2. 02 Open reliable, relevant primary or authoritative sources.
  3. 03 Confirm that each source supports the exact claim.
  4. 04 Compare conflicting information and note uncertainty.
  5. 05 Correct, qualify, or remove unsupported content.
